Welcome to Classroom++

By Jonathan Kung

Turn the Google Classroom Classwork feed into an organized, scrollable workspace — rearrange posts without dragging — and mark a whole batch of assignments reviewed from the To review page in one go.

Chrome will ask for access to classroom.google.com once. Classroom++ only reads the page you are already on, and never sends anything anywhere.

Check out what's new in the Changelog

5-Post Scroll Windows

Every topic is capped to 5 visible posts with its own smooth scroll area. All of a topic's posts are loaded up front, so nothing hides behind Google's "View more".

Smart Draft & Post Centering

Automatically scrolls to the boundary where your latest published post meets upcoming drafts, so you can pick up editing right where you left off.

One-Click "View all" Toggle

Need to see everything in a topic at once? Click "View all" to expand the full topic feed, and "View fewer" to return to 5 posts.

Reverse Order

Flip the order of posts in every topic with one click. The button sits right next to Google's own "Collapse all" and matches its style.

Move Posts Without Dragging

Hover a post for an up arrow, a down arrow and a topic dot beside it. Step the post one place at a time — the ends wrap around — or open the dot to send it to another topic.

Bulk Select on To Review

A checkbox on every assignment plus Select all and a floating bar, so a batch can be marked reviewed in one pass. Shift-click selects a whole range.

Four Separate Switches

Scroll areas, the move overlay, the Reverse order button and Bulk Select each have their own switch in the extension popup. Changes apply immediately and sync across your Chrome browsers.
